# Uploads

Put photos, documents and other material for your pages here: a menu as a PDF, photos of your work, a price list, a team photo. Claude reads them while writing each page and copies what the site needs into the right place. Nothing in this folder is published on its own.

To add files on GitHub's website: open this folder, click **Add file** → **Upload files**, drag the files in, then click **Commit changes**. Tell Claude which page they're for.

Good to know:
- **Photos:** JPG or PNG, at least 1200 pixels wide. Under about 1 MB each is best; Claude will ask for a smaller version if one is very large.
- **Documents:** PDF. Claude puts the important facts from a document on the page as text too, so visitors and search engines can read them without opening the file.
- Only upload photos you have the right to use, and of people who are happy to appear on your site.

Logos and brand guidelines go in `brand-files/` instead.
