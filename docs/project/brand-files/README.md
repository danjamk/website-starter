# Brand files

Put your logo, brand guidelines, photos of printed material, or anything else that shows your brand here. Claude reads them during the brand interview and copies what the site needs into the right place. Nothing in this folder is published on your site.

To add files on GitHub's website: open this folder, click **Add file** → **Upload files**, drag the files in, then click **Commit changes**.

Best formats:
- **Logo:** SVG if you have it. Otherwise a PNG with a transparent background, at least 512 pixels wide.
- **Guidelines:** PDF.
- **Fonts:** only if you bought a font that isn't freely available. Check its license allows use on a website.
