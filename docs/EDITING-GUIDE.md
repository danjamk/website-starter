# Working on your website with Claude

You change your site by telling Claude what you want, in your own words. There's no editor to learn. This page explains how that works.

## Starting a session

1. Go to claude.ai/code.
2. Below the message box, pick your site and the environment you set up.
3. Say what you want. If you're not sure, say **"let's keep going"** and Claude suggests the next step from your plan.

## Asking for changes

Describe what you want as you'd tell a person:

- "Change the headline to 'Fresh bread every morning'."
- "Add our Saturday hours to the contact page: 8 to 2."
- "The blue is too bright. Make it darker."
- "Add a page about our catering service."

Claude asks if anything is unclear. You don't need to know where things are on the site.

## Seeing a change before anyone else does

Claude makes every change on a draft first and gives you a **preview link**. The preview looks exactly like your site will, but only people with the link can find it. Look at it on your phone too.

Then tell Claude:

- **"Publish it"** (or "yes, put it live") to put it on your real site. It's live within about a minute.
- **What to change** if it's not right. The preview link stays the same and updates.
- **"Not now"** to leave it as a draft. Claude remembers it for next time.

Nothing goes on your real site until you say so.

## Undoing a change

Say **"undo that"** or **"put the site back to how it was before …"**. Claude shows you what it will undo and asks before doing it. Nothing is ever lost: earlier versions are kept, so a change you undo can come back later.

## Useful phrases

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "let's keep going" | Claude reads your plan and suggests the next step |
| "is everything OK?" | Claude checks the site and tells you about anything that needs attention |
| "publish it" | A change you've seen on a preview goes on your live site |
| "undo that" | Claude puts the site back to how it was before a change |
| "are there any updates?" | Claude checks for improvements to the starter site and explains them |

## Your plan

Your plan is a list of steps, about 30 minutes each, that Claude keeps up to date. You can stop at any point, even partway through a question, and pick up next time. Ask Claude to show you the plan whenever you like.

## What Claude won't do without asking you first

- Put anything on your live site.
- Add anything that sends your visitors' information to another company, such as tracking, chat widgets or embedded videos.
- Spend money or sign you up for anything.
- Delete your words or pages.

## When you need a person

Some things are beyond what Claude can do on its own, such as online payments, logins for customers, or problems with your web address. Claude tells you when that's the case. Whoever helps you should read `RUNBOOK.md`, in the same folder as this guide.

## Keep in mind

- Never give Claude passwords or account numbers. If you do by mistake, change that password.
- Your site's files, your plan and your answers are private. Only the website itself is public.
